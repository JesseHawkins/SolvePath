# Settings {#guide.settings}

Settings let you change how SolvePath looks and works.

## Theme {#settings.appearance.theme}

The theme changes the app's colors.

- **Dark:** dark background, easy on the eyes. This is the starting theme.
- **Light:** light background, good in a bright room.
- **High contrast:** black and white with bright colors, for the clearest possible view.

Every theme is checked to make sure text is easy to read.

## Arrows {#settings.cube.arrows}

When the cube shows you a move one at a time, an arrow shows which way it turns. You can pick the kind of arrow on the **Cube** page:

- **Section arrows** (the default): a straight arrow lies on the part of the cube that moves and points the way it goes. For R, the arrow runs up the right column of the front face.
- **Face arrows:** a circle sits on the face that turns and goes the way that face turns, as if you were looking straight at it. For R, it's a clockwise circle on the right face.

Face arrows are only for the six outer faces. Two-layer moves (like r) and middle moves (M, E, S) always get straight arrows, and turning the whole cube always gets the big curved arrow.

Every circle lies flat on its own face. You can't see the left, bottom, or back face from the normal view, so for those faces you also get the straight arrow on a face you can see. To see the circle itself, press [Inspect](#feature.inspect) and spin the cube around.

Your choice is saved on this device.
