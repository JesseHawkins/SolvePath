# Privacy {#guide.privacy}

We built SolvePath for families, so we keep as little information as we can.

## The camera {#privacy.camera}

SolvePath uses your camera only to read the colors on your cube.

- Video is **never recorded, saved, or uploaded**.
- Each camera picture is looked at once, inside your device, and then thrown away.
- The camera only turns on when you start a scan, and it turns off when you're done.

## Your data {#privacy.data}

Without an account, everything stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

There are no ads, no trackers, and no analytics. The app never collects a child's real name, email, birthday, or photo.

## Keeping your data safe {#privacy.backup}

Some browsers (especially Safari on iPhone and iPad) can delete a website's saved data if you don't visit it for a while. To keep your progress safe, you can add SolvePath to your Home Screen, or make a backup file from Settings.
