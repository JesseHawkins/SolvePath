# Glossary {#guide.glossary}

Words you'll see in SolvePath, explained simply.

## Face {#glossary.face}

One side of the cube. A 3x3 cube has 6 faces.

## Sticker {#glossary.sticker}

One colored square on a face. Each face has 9 stickers, so the whole cube has 54. Some cubes have no real stickers and use colored plastic instead. We still call each square a sticker.

## Scramble {#glossary.scramble}

A list of turns that mixes up a solved cube. Everyone who does the same scramble gets the same mixed-up cube.

## Algorithm {#glossary.algorithm}

A list of turns that does one job, like moving a few pieces while keeping the rest in place. Cubers often say "alg" for short.

## Moves and notation {#glossary.notation}

Cubers write moves with letters. Each letter is a face, as you look at the cube:

- **U** is up (the top), **D** is down (the bottom).
- **R** is right, **L** is left.
- **F** is front (the side facing you), **B** is back.

A letter on its own means turn that face a quarter turn clockwise, as if you were looking right at that face. Add **'** (say "prime") to turn it the other way. Add **2** to turn it twice.

There are a few more kinds of moves:

- A lowercase letter, like **r**, turns two layers together: the face and the middle layer next to it. You may also see this written **Rw**.
- **M**, **E**, and **S** turn just a middle layer. M turns the same way as L, E turns the same way as D, and S turns the same way as F.
- **x**, **y**, and **z** turn the whole cube in your hands. x turns the same way as R, y the same way as U, and z the same way as F.
