# Welcome {#guide.welcome}

SolvePath helps you learn, solve, and speedcube with any 3x3 puzzle cube. It runs in your web browser, so there's nothing to download.

## Home {#feature.home}

The Home screen is where you start. As we build the app, this is where you'll find scanning, solving, lessons, and the timer.

## The Guide {#feature.guide}

This Guide explains every part of the app in plain words. Anywhere in the app, a name with a dotted underline is a link. Tap it to jump straight to its Guide entry.

You can also search the Guide. Type a word like "theme" and pick a result.

The Guide works even when you're offline.
