# Virtual cube {#guide.virtual-cube}

A cube on the screen that you can turn, just like a real one.

## The virtual cube {#feature.virtual-cube}

Open **Cube** at the top of the page. You can turn the cube with your finger, your mouse, the keyboard, or the turn buttons.

The box next to the cube tells you when it's solved. Turning the whole cube doesn't count against you: a solved cube held any way is still solved.

- **Scramble** mixes the cube up with a random scramble, the same kind used in competitions.
- **Undo** takes back your last move.
- **Reset** puts the cube back to solved.

## Turning the cube {#cube.controls}

**Swipe** a row or column of stickers to turn it that way. Swipe the middle row to turn the middle slice.

**Turn the whole cube** by swiping in the empty space around it. With a mouse, you can also drag anywhere with the right button, or hold **Shift** and drag. Swipe sideways to turn it like y, or up and down to turn it like x. For z, use the buttons or the keys.

**Turn buttons** come in three groups:

- **Turn one layer:** the six faces and the three middle layers (M, E, S).
- **Turn two layers:** a face and the middle layer next to it, written with a small letter. For example, r is R and M' together.
- **Turn the whole cube:** x, y, and z. These have an outline so they stand out, because no pieces move.

**Keyboard:** click or tap the cube first, then press these keys. They're the same keys many speedcubing sites use, so your fingers may already know them.

## Inspect {#feature.inspect}

Press **Inspect** in the corner of the cube to look at it from any side. Drag to spin it freely in any direction. You can also see the faces that are usually hidden (left, bottom, and back).

Inspecting only changes how you look at the cube. It doesn't turn the cube in your hands, so the letters still mean the faces from your normal view: R is still the face on your right when you look the normal way. Arrows and highlights stay stuck to the cube, so they always point at the part that will really turn.

You can still make moves while inspecting, with the keys or the buttons. Swipes spin the view instead of turning layers.

Press **Inspect** again (or **Esc**) to go back. The cube glides back to the normal view.

## Trying moves {#cube.tryMoves}

Type some moves in the box, like `R U R' U'`, then press **Do them**. To learn what the letters mean, see [Moves and notation](#glossary.notation).

Press **One at a time** to go slowly. An arrow shows which way the next move turns. Press **Next move** to do it.

- When part of the cube turns, a straight arrow lies on the part that moves. That part stays bright while the rest goes dim. Two-layer moves get a wider arrow.
- When the whole cube turns, a big curved arrow wraps around the cube.
- Half turns (like R2) have two arrowheads.

You can switch the arrows for face turns to circles. See [Arrows](#settings.cube.arrows).
